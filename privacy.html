Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

The short version: your vaping data stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We don't have accounts, we don't sell data, we don't show ads, and we don't use your habits for marketing.

What Unpuff stores

Unpuff saves the following only on your device:

  • Puff entries (time, quantity, and where you logged it: app, widget, Watch or Siri)
  • Cravings (time, how strong, outcome, coping strategy)
  • Optional triggers, moods and notes you add
  • Goals, plans and settings
  • Optional device details (nicotine strength, liquid size, puffs per device, price)

Unpuff has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or health records.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on sync or backup, your data is stored in your own private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit. We can't see it. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. Turn sync off anytime in Settings.

Purchases

Unpuff Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment. We only receive confirmation that a subscription is active, never your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders, such as a spacing goal or a trial ending, are scheduled on your device. You can turn each one off in Unpuff's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Analytics and third parties

Unpuff contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs, and doesn't track you across apps or websites. If we add crash reporting, it will be anonymous, won't include your logs, and this policy will be updated first.

Accountability partners

If a partner feature becomes available, nothing is shared unless you invite someone. By default a partner sees only general progress, such as a percentage drop or a streak. Exact puff counts are shared only with your explicit permission.

Your control

  • Export: Settings → Export CSV.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete all data erases everything on the device, and in iCloud if sync is on. Deleting the app also removes local data.

Because we don't hold your data on our servers, we can't access, recover or disclose it. Your rights under laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the GDPR and the CCPA are respected by design.

Age

Unpuff is meant for adults of legal age to buy vaping products where they live. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Not medical advice

Nicotine figures are estimates. Unpuff doesn't diagnose, treat or replace advice from a health professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and note the change in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a request.
