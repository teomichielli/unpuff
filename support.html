Common questions

Is Unpuff free?

Yes. Unlimited puff tracking, cravings, daily goals, hourly and weekly charts, widgets, Apple Watch, Siri and data export are free forever. Unpuff Pro is optional and adds deeper insights, adaptive plans, full history, backup and cloud skins.

How do I cancel or manage Pro?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Unpuff. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal or trial ends to avoid being charged.

How do I get a refund?

Apple processes all refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing.

Open Unpuff → Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with.

My puff count looks wrong.

Every puff is saved as its own timestamped entry, and your count is calculated from those entries. Check Today → Today's log: tap × to remove a mistaken entry, or use Undo right after logging.

The widget or Watch isn't updating.

Make sure Unpuff is updated on both devices, then remove and re-add the widget. For the Watch, open the Unpuff Watch app once while your iPhone is nearby.

How accurate is the nicotine estimate?

It's a rough estimate: strength × liquid ÷ puffs per device, using the details you enter under Settings → Your vape. How much nicotine your body absorbs varies a lot, so use it to see trends, not as a dose.

How do I export or delete my data?

Settings → Export CSV saves a copy of your log. Settings → Delete all data permanently erases everything on your device. If you turned on iCloud sync, it's erased there too.

Need to quit?

Unpuff can help you cut down, but a health professional can do more. In Canada, call 1-866-366-3667 (Canada's free quit line) or visit canada.ca/quit. In the US, call 1-800-QUIT-NOW.
